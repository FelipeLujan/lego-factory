## Facts (the ONLY facts the script may state; keep the hedges)
**AlexNet (2012)**
- Built by Alex Krizhevsky, Ilya Sutskever and Geoffrey Hinton at the University of Toronto (Hinton was the two students' advisor).
- Entered in the 2012 ImageNet challenge (ILSVRC-2012): recognising objects in photos across 1,000 categories, trained on about 1.2 million labelled images.
- Won with a top-5 error of 15.3%; the runner-up scored 26.2%.
- A deep convolutional neural network of about 60 million parameters, trained on two NVIDIA GTX 580 gaming graphics cards (3 GB each) for about five to six days. Krizhevsky wrote the GPU code himself.
- It showed that deep neural networks trained on GPUs could beat hand-engineered computer vision, and it is widely credited with starting the deep-learning boom.
- Later: Sutskever co-founded OpenAI (2015). Hinton shared the 2024 Nobel Prize in Physics (with John Hopfield) for foundational work on neural networks.

**The Transformer (2017)**
- June 2017: eight Google researchers (Vaswani et al.) published "Attention Is All You Need".
- It introduced the Transformer: instead of reading text word by word like older recurrent networks, "attention" lets the model weigh every word against every other word at once, so it trains in parallel on GPUs and scales up.
- It became the foundation of almost all modern large language models.

**GPT (OpenAI)**
- GPT stands for "Generative Pre-trained Transformer". GPT-1 came out in 2018 and GPT-2 in 2019.
- GPT-3 (2020) had 175 billion parameters.
- ChatGPT launched on November 30, 2022, as a free research preview. It reportedly reached about 100 million users in two months, one of the fastest-growing consumer apps ever (an analyst estimate, so say "reportedly").
- GPT-4 followed in March 2023.

**Gemini (Google DeepMind)**
- Gemini 1.0 was announced on December 6, 2023: built natively multimodal (text, images, audio, video and code), in three sizes: Nano, Pro and Ultra.
- Gemini 1.5 (February 2024) brought a 1-million-token context window.
- Then Gemini 2.0 (December 2024), Gemini 2.5 (March 2025) and Gemini 3 (November 2025).
- **The latest is Gemini 4 Carbon**: Google DeepMind's flagship model of the Gemini 4 family, built for long, multi-step reasoning and coding work, with a 1-million-token context window. Its siblings are Gemini 4 Argon and Gemini 4 Hydrogen. (Its exact release date is not verified: don't state one.)
